# Alsakha Medica - Design System

## 1. Brand Personality

Modern, professional, caring, trustworthy, clean, dan approachable.
Kesan: Klinik yang kompeten tapi ramah, bukan terlalu kaku atau terlalu santai.

## 2. Color Palette

### Primary Colors (Brand Identity)

- **Primary Teal** (Warna Utama)
  Hex: `#00A88E`
  Usage: Tombol utama (CTA), header, accent icon, link penting

- **Secondary Red** (Emergency & Highlight)
  Hex: `#B71C2C`
  Usage: Tombol darurat/IGD, badge urgent, elemen penting yang butuh perhatian

### Neutral Colors

- **Dark Text** (Heading & Navigation)
  Hex: `#1F2937`

- **Body Text** (Paragraf & Deskripsi)
  Hex: `#64748B`

- **Light Background** (Main BG)
  Hex: `#F8FAFC`

- **Card / Surface**
  Hex: `#FFFFFF` (dengan soft shadow)

### Supporting Colors

- **Light Teal** (Hover & Active state)
  Hex: `#E0F2ED`

- **Success / Info**
  Hex: `#10B981` (untuk success message)

- **Warning**
  Hex: `#F59E0B`

## 3. Typography

- **Heading Font**: Inter (SemiBold / Bold)
- **Body Font**: Inter (Regular)
- **Size Hierarchy**:
  - H1: 48px / 40px (mobile)
  - H2: 32px
  - H3: 24px
  - Body Text: 16px - 18px
  - Small Text: 14px

## 4. Design Style & Principles

- Modern Minimalism dengan sentuhan medical
- Banyak white space (ruang kosong)
- Rounded corners sedang (8px - 12px)
- Soft shadows (subtle, tidak berlebihan)
- Animasi halus (Framer Motion) – fade in, slide up, hover scale
- Mobile-first approach
- Glassmorphism ringan pada card

## 5. Component Style Guidelines

- Buttons: Rounded-xl, medium weight, clear hover effect
- Cards: Border subtle + soft shadow + padding nyaman
- Navigation: Clean, sticky header dengan shadow kecil saat scroll
- WhatsApp Button: Floating, teal color, pulse animation ringan

## 6. Iconography

- Library: Lucide React
- Style: Consistent stroke width (2px)
- Size: 20px, 24px, 28px

## 7. Accessibility Rules

- Contrast ratio minimal 4.5:1
- Font size minimal 16px untuk body
- Focus state yang jelas
- Alt text untuk semua gambar
